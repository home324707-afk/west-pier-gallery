AI works in the exhibition (104)

These views were made by the West Pier Curator (an AI) as live generative programs (ai/pieces.js and the families in ai/fam/), with still poster frames rendered by tools/ai_art. They are deliberately stylised and are not documentary images of the pier. Wholly original pieces are released under CC0. Pieces derived from gallery images only use public-domain, CC0 and CC BY sources. They are released under CC BY 4.0 where a source requires attribution, and the original artists are credited below. No share-alike, non-commercial or no-derivatives image was used as a source this time. We also searched Wikimedia Commons and Openverse for openly licensed AI-generated images of the West Pier to hang as "found AI art" and found none (25 October 2026).

At the curator's request the AI side was brought up to parity with the human side in session 6 (25 October 2026), across 18 families: Flow fields (3), L-systems (3), Cellular automata (4), Tide simulations (3), Typography (3), Writing (11), Remixes (9), After the masters (9), Plotter drawings (3), Sound pieces (3), Interactive (6), Turing test failures (6), Made by hand (by a machine) (10), Specimens & instruments (6), 3D & maps (4), Games & puzzles (4), Minimal & conceptual (3), Data portraits (3). The remixes use only public-domain and CC0 sources, all credited below. The "after the masters" pieces are homages painted from code; no image by those painters is used. The data portraits use only this gallery's catalogue and the sourced timeline.

Every output is shown unedited. The only change is that code was lifted out of its Markdown fence. The model name is the one the service returned, not the one we asked for. Services were free and needed no account, and their terms were checked before publishing. Code by guests runs only in a sandboxed frame with no network access. Image models were tried as well. None is shown here: the only free, keyless image model reachable (Sana, via Pollinations.ai) returned photographic images every time, even when asked for flat illustration, and this gallery does not hang photoreal AI images.
