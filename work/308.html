A fabricated photograph. This picture looks like a photograph but is not one. It was generated by FLUX.1-schnell, an image model, from the text prompt below. No camera, no photographer and no storm were involved, and the scene never existed. Please do not pass it on as a real or news photograph of the pier. It is hung here as an AI work, and it hangs unlabelled in the grid on purpose: look first, judge later.
