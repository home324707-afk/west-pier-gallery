Artist

Method
Derivative mosaic. No. 39 rebuilt from 32-pixel tiles of 31 gallery views (Nos. 1, 7, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24, 26, 39, 43, 44, 45, 46, 49, 50, 51, 64, 67, 69, 72, 75, 80, 89, 91, 95, 96, 101), each tinted towards the colour it replaces. Only public-domain, CC0 and CC BY views were used as tiles. Python, NumPy.
