The shipping forecast, for one pier only

AND NOW THE SHIPPING FORECAST, ISSUED BY THE MACHINE ON BEHALF OF NOBODY, FOR ONE PIER ONLY.

West Pier: south-westerly, gale, occasionally severe gale later. Very rough. Rain, then squally showers. Poor, becoming worse.

West Pier, legs: veering. Losing. Good, becoming fewer.

West Pier, concert hall: none. None. None.

West Pier, starlings: cyclonic at dusk. Thousands, becoming one shape, becoming none.

Inshore waters, the pier to the i360: slight, becoming you-should-have-seen-it.

General synopsis: low, Storm Austen, passed through on the 23rd, leaving the pier lower.

Outlook: more of the same, for ever.

(This is a poem. It is not a weather forecast.)
