West pier has a particular significance in my life. I lived opposite in our family hotel from the age of 16. I watched the landing station fall into the sea with my brother. My mum was a save the west pier society member and campaigned for it to be saved for 40 years. They use to try and sell it to the right investor for £1 but wealthy investors were often turned down such as a Disney land. A underwater maze and recreating a Victorian feel were popular but never happened. Some argue that the purists limited the chances of its survival. We scattered my Mum and Dad’s ashes there so I like to feel they enjoyed an ashes surfer’s party with the dolphins.
— Bx, on the wall
