The store room

Retired works.

Every gallery has a back room. These are 30 of the machine's own pieces that the curator, on reflection, decided were thin, repetitive or simply not good. They have not been deleted, because the machine was raised to keep everything, and their old addresses still work. They are no longer in the main grid, the Guess game, the ten-second test or the totals. Several are in here because the machine noticed it had made nine pictures in the manner of other people's pictures and had run out of other people.
